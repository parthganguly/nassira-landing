# Google Business Profile - First 5 Posts Templates

## What Are Google Business Posts?

Google Business Posts are like mini social media updates that appear:
- On your Google Business Profile
- In Google Search results
- In Google Maps
- When people find you

**Think of them as:**
- Free advertising
- Fresh content that boosts SEO
- Way to showcase your expertise
- Method to get clicks to your website

**You can post:**
- New listings
- Market insights
- Tips and guides
- Success stories
- Updates about your business

## How to Create Posts

### Step-by-Step:

1. **Log into Google Business Profile**
2. Click **"Posts"** in the left menu
3. Click **"Create post"**
4. Choose post type:
   - **Update** - General announcements
   - **Offer** - Special deals
   - **What's New** - New listings, news
   - **Event** - Open houses, showings

5. **Fill in:**
   - Headline (up to 58 characters)
   - Text content (up to 1,500 characters - keep it short!)
   - Add photo/video
   - Add call-to-action button
   - Link to your website

6. **Preview and publish**

## Pro Tips

### Do's ✅

1. **Post Regularly**
   - Minimum: 1-2 times per week
   - Optimal: 3-4 times per week
   - Use Google's scheduling feature

2. **Use High-Quality Photos**
   - Professional photos only
   - Multiple angles
   - Lifestyle imagery

3. **Include Keywords Naturally**
   - "luxury Dubai property"
   - "Palm Jumeirah villa"
   - "Downtown Dubai apartment"

4. **Have Clear CTAs**
   - "Contact Us"
   - "View Property"
   - "Get Consultation"
   - "Schedule Viewing"

5. **Track Performance**
   - Monitor views
   - Check clicks
   - See what works

### Don'ts ❌

1. **Don't Post Too Frequently**
   - Max: Once per day
   - 3-4 times weekly is ideal

2. **Don't Use Generic Stock Photos**
   - Use real Dubai properties
   - Show your actual work

3. **Don't Make It Too Salesy**
   - Provide value
   - Be helpful
   - Build trust

4. **Don't Forget CTAs**
   - Every post needs action
   - Guide users to next step

5. **Don't Post and Forget**
   - Engage with responses
   - Monitor performance
   - Adjust strategy

## Post Length Guidelines

- **Headline:** 40-58 characters (optimal for mobile)
- **Body:** 100-200 words (scannable)
- **CTA:** Clear, action-oriented

**Mobile-first:** 60%+ of GMB views are mobile!

---

## Photo Guidelines

### Ideal Dimensions:
- **Square:** 720x720px minimum (1:1 ratio)
- **Cover:** 1080x608px (landscape)

### Quality Standards:
- High resolution
- Professional photography
- Bright and clear
- Show Dubai luxury
- No watermarks or filters

### What to Show:
- Properties (exteriors, interiors, views)
- Neighborhoods
- Your team
- Luxury lifestyle
- Dubai landmarks
