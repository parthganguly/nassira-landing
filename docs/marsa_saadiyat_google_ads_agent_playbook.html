Generate qualified Marsa Al Saadiyat enquiries without making the brand look like a generic off-plan broker.

Campaign principle: the ad, the landing page, the WhatsApp experience and Nassira's public brand must feel like one system.
Primary goalHigh-intent WhatsApp leads for Marsa / Saadiyat off-plan interest.

04 · Execution Sequence

Follow these phases in order

0

Freeze the truth sheet

  • Research latest Marsa announcement from Aldar / Abu Dhabi official sources.
  • Record what is known, unknown and newly announced.
  • Mark every numerical claim with a source.
  • Confirm whether official project renders exist for the exact residential release.
Gate: no copywriting or image generation until this is complete.
1

Lock the Nassira visual system

  • Headline type: EB Garamond.
  • Body/UI type: Geist.
  • Primary surfaces: white, black, warm neutral.
  • Accent: copper #c17a5c.
  • Use rounded editorial image cards and restrained overlays.
  • Avoid navy/gold "Dubai brochure" styling.
Gate: creative director must be able to place the ad beside NassiraProperties.com and have it visibly belong.
2

Create three campaign concepts

  1. The Next Saadiyat — anticipation / new chapter.
  2. Waterfront, Reimagined — marina / beach / lifestyle.
  3. Enter Before the Launch — high-intent registration.

Each concept gets one master still and one 15–20 sec video treatment before adaptation.

Gate: choose 1–2 winners before generating a full asset library.
3

Produce stills + motion

  • Use official renders when referring to exact project architecture.
  • Use campaign atmosphere only for generic Saadiyat lifestyle.
  • Never generate a fictitious building and present it as Marsa.
  • Human scenes should feel editorial/documentary, not posed AI luxury clichés.
  • Keep text inside creative minimal; Google ad copy carries detail.
4

Build landing experience

Marsa becomes the lead story for Saadiyat new launches. Existing opportunities such as Mandarin Oriental can remain lower on the page.

Gate: ad headline, page headline and WhatsApp prefill must communicate the same offer.
5

Wire conversion tracking

  • Google Ads WhatsApp click conversion.
  • Phone click conversion if used.
  • UTM persistence.
  • gclid / gbraid / wbraid persistence where applicable.
  • Test from ad click → landing → WhatsApp.
Gate: campaigns cannot launch until test conversions are visible.
6

Launch Search first, Demand Gen second

Search captures explicit Marsa/Saadiyat intent. Demand Gen distributes the strongest visual concept across eligible Google surfaces.

7

Optimize for qualified conversations

Do not judge creative purely on CTR. The real metric is whether WhatsApp conversations resemble genuine buyers.

05 · Creative System

What the ads should look and feel like

Do
  • Large photographic moments.
  • Editorial restraint.
  • Warm ivory / black / copper.
  • EB Garamond statement headlines.
  • Real-world motion: water, boats, arrival, walking, dining, doors opening, natural wind.
  • Architecture as context, not CGI spectacle.
Do not
  • Gold foil titles over navy gradients.
  • Fake skyline composites.
  • Yachts used as lazy shorthand for wealth in every frame.
  • Perfect AI couples staring into sunsets.
  • Overfilled collage layouts.
  • Huge blocks of embedded text.

Concept copy starting points

CONCEPT A MARSA AL SAADIYAT The next chapter of Saadiyat. First residential releases expected H2 2026. Register your interest with Nassira Properties.
CONCEPT B 8 KM OF WATERFRONT. A NEW SIDE OF SAADIYAT. Marsa Al Saadiyat. Register for launch information.
CONCEPT C FIRST HOMES. H2 2026. Marsa Al Saadiyat. Speak directly with Nassira for current launch information.
06 · Magnific Production Workflow

Generate with control, not one-shot prompts

  1. Start from the approved creative concept and site reference board.
  2. Prepare 4–6 visual references: Nassira website screenshots, official Saadiyat/Marsa imagery, approved lifestyle references.
  3. Create hero stills first. Do not start video until one still is brand-correct.
  4. For video, create separate short clips with a specific shot purpose.
  5. Assemble clips only after each shot passes visual QA.
  6. Upscale final output only after content and motion are locked.

Recommended 15–20 second shot structure

TimeShotPurpose
0–3sWater / shoreline / arrivalImmediate Saadiyat atmosphere
3–6sMarina movement / boat glide / promenadeEnergy and destination scale
6–10sArchitecture / terrace / detailLuxury product cue
10–14sHuman lifestyle momentEmotional connection
14–18sClean brand/title frameMarsa + Nassira CTA
Visual disclosure rule: if the architecture is generated for atmosphere and not an official Marsa render, do not label the generated structure as an exact Marsa residence.

B. Demand Gen — after creative winner exists

  • Run only approved Marsa creative concepts.
  • Use channel controls where useful to isolate YouTube / Shorts / Discover performance.
  • Create separate 16:9, 9:16, 1:1 and 4:5 assets rather than lazy crops.

Negative keyword themes

Jobs, careers, free, rent (if campaign is sales-only), hotel booking, restaurant, beach club, map, wallpaper, images, history, school project, PDF download unless deliberately targeted.

Do not begin with Performance Max. Introduce it later only when conversion quality and creative control are understood.
08 · Landing Page

Required message flow

  1. Hero: Marsa Al Saadiyat + concise launch timing + WhatsApp CTA.
  2. Why it matters: waterfront, beaches, marina, scale.
  3. What is known now: verified facts only.
  4. What is not yet released: pricing / layouts / exact first release — invite enquiry for updates.
  5. Why Nassira: founder visibility, Million Dollar Listing credibility, direct communication.
  6. Existing Saadiyat opportunity: Mandarin Oriental / other genuine current inventory below Marsa.
  7. Final CTA: WhatsApp with prefilled Marsa message.
WhatsApp prefill: Hello Nassira, I'm interested in Marsa Al Saadiyat. Please send me the latest launch information and let me know when the first residential release becomes available.

10 · QA Gates

Definition of done

Creative QA

  • Looks like Nassira Properties, not a generic property portal.
  • EB Garamond / Geist hierarchy is respected.
  • Copper accent is restrained.
  • No fake building is represented as official Marsa architecture.
  • No AI anatomy, reflection, signage or text errors.
  • No unsupported commercial claims.

Landing QA

  • Mobile-first layout passes visual review.
  • WhatsApp opens correctly with Marsa prefill.
  • Contact details are correct.
  • Marsa facts match the latest truth sheet.
  • Ad message and landing hero message match.

Ads QA

  • Correct final URL and UTMs.
  • Conversion action fires once per intended action.
  • No misleading urgency or guaranteed returns.
  • Search terms / negatives reviewed after launch.
  • Lead quality is reviewed, not just CPC/CTR.
Launch condition: research, creative, landing and tracking gates must all be green before paid traffic is enabled.
