# How to Publish Posts on Google Business Profile

## Step-by-Step Guide

### Step 1: Access Your Google Business Profile

**Option A: Via Google Search**
1. Go to [google.com](https://www.google.com)
2. Search for "Nassira Properties"
3. In the right panel (Knowledge Panel), look for "Manage" or your business name
4. Click on it - this opens your dashboard

**Option B: Direct Access**
1. Go to [business.google.com](https://business.google.com)
2. Sign in with your Google account
3. Select "Nassira Properties" from your businesses

---

### Step 2: Navigate to Posts

**In your Google Business Profile dashboard:**

1. Look at the left sidebar menu
2. Find and click **"Posts"** 
   - It might show as a document icon or "Posts" text
   - It's usually near the top of the menu

**Alternative:**
- You might see a button that says **"Create post"** or **"New post"** directly on the dashboard
- Click that if you see it

---

### Step 3: Create Your First Post

**Once you're in the Posts section:**

1. Click **"Create post"** or **"+ New post"** button
   - Usually a blue button in the top right

2. **Choose Post Type:**
   - **Update** - General announcements (use this for welcome post)
   - **Offer** - Special deals or promotions
   - **What's New** - New listings, news
   - **Event** - Open houses, showings

   **For your first post, choose "Update"**

---

### Step 4: Fill in Post Details

**You'll see a form with these fields:**

1. **Add Photo/Video** (important!)
   - Click "Add photo" or drag & drop
   - Upload: `public/images/luxury-villa-pool-sunset.png` or similar
   - Use a high-quality image (720x720px minimum)

2. **Write Post**
   - **Headline:** Enter title (up to 58 characters)
   - **Text:** Write your content (up to 1,500 characters)
   - **Note:** Keep it concise - 100-200 words works best

3. **Add Call-to-Action Button**
   - Choose button type:
     - "Learn more" → Link to your About page
     - "Call now" → Direct phone call
     - "Get quote" → Contact form
     - "Visit website" → Homepage
   - Enter the URL (e.g., `https://www.nassiraproperties.com/about`)

---

### Step 5: Preview and Publish

1. **Review your post:**
   - Check spelling and grammar
   - Make sure photo looks good
   - Verify the CTA link works

2. **Click "Publish"** or **"Post"** button
   - Usually blue button at bottom right

3. **Success!** Your post is now live
   - It will appear in:
     - Your Google Business Profile
     - Google Search results
     - Google Maps

## Quick Tips

### Photo Guidelines:
- **Size:** Minimum 720x720px (square) or 1080x608px (landscape)
- **Format:** JPG or PNG
- **Quality:** High resolution, professional photos
- **Content:** Show luxury properties, Dubai landmarks, or your team

### Text Guidelines:
- **Headline:** 40-58 characters (optimal for mobile)
- **Body:** 100-200 words (keep it scannable)
- **Keywords:** Naturally include "luxury Dubai property", "Palm Jumeirah", etc.
- **Tone:** Professional but warm, value-focused

### Timing:
- **Post #1:** Today
- **Post #2:** In 2-3 days
- **Post #3:** In 4-5 days
- **Going forward:** 2-3 posts per week

## Troubleshooting

### "I can't find the Posts option"
- Make sure you're logged into the correct Google account
- Check if you're viewing the business (not just search results)
- Try accessing via business.google.com directly

### "I can't upload photos"
- Check file size (should be under 5MB)
- Try different image format (JPG instead of PNG)
- Make sure image is at least 720x720px

### "Post didn't publish"
- Check internet connection
- Try refreshing the page
- Make sure all required fields are filled
- Check if there are any error messages

### "I don't see my post in search"
- Posts can take 24-48 hours to appear in search results
- They'll show up immediately on your Google Business Profile
- Make sure you're searching from the same account
